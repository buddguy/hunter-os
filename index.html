🎯 How streak works: Complete 2 out of 3 sections (Gym, Uni, Skills) in a single day to earn a streak day. Just opening the app doesn't count — you have to actually do the work.

🔥 7-Day Burst: Hit 7 consecutive streak days = +100 XP explosion + the streak counter resets and climbs again.

⚡ XP Multiplier: Higher rank = more XP per action. A-rank Hunter earns 2.5× more than E-rank on every task.

⚠️ Miss a day: Streak resets to 0. You keep your XP and rank, but you can't rank up without rebuilding your streak.

📋 XP per action: Daily task = +5 XP · Sport session = +12 XP · Gym session = +20 XP · Sub-goal = +15 XP · 7-day burst = +80 XP · (all multiplied by your rank multiplier)
